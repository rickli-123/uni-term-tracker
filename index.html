Template-based (~255 schools): Schools sharing a calendar pattern with a verified peer (e.g., other Cal State campuses use the same dates as Cal State LA; other UC quarter campuses match UCLA). These should be accurate within ±1 week but may have small term-specific differences.

Coverage: All R1/R2 universities, flagship publics, major privates, large community college districts, and notable specialty schools across the 25 most populous U.S. metro areas (325 institutions). Community college districts are listed as a single row when member campuses share one calendar (LACCD, CUNY, Maricopa, Dallas College, etc.).
